# User profiles, departments and designations for Indian schools

Researched 17 Nov 2026 from school staff lists, Kendriya Vidyalaya / Navodaya post names and school job postings.
The app ships the first seven as built-in profiles. The rest are in **Roles & Permissions → User management → Standard profiles for Indian schools**, and each can be added with one click.

| Department | Profile | Designations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administration | Admin | School Administrator, Administrative Officer, Office Manager, IT Administrator |
| School Leadership | Principal | Principal, Vice Principal, Headmistress / Headmaster, Academic Director |
| Academics | Teacher | Pre-Primary Teacher (PPRT / NTT), PRT, TGT, PGT, Class Teacher, Coordinator, Head of Department, Head of Teaching Staff, Vice Principal, Physical Education Teacher, Art & Craft Teacher, Music Teacher, Dance Teacher, Computer Teacher, Special Educator, Counsellor, Librarian, Lab Assistant |
| Accounts & Finance | Accountant | Accounts Manager, Accountant, Senior Accountant, Accounts Assistant, Cashier, Fee Clerk, HR & Payroll Executive |
| Front Office & Admissions | Front Office | Receptionist, Front Office Executive, Admissions Officer, Admission Counsellor, Office Superintendent, Senior Secretariat Assistant (UDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (LDC), Office Assistant, Data Entry Operator, Gate / Security Staff |
| Transport | Transport team | Transport In-charge, Transport Supervisor, Driver, Cleaner, Bus Helper / Attendant |
| Parents & Guardians | Parent | (none — marked Parent) |
| Library | Librarian | Librarian, Assistant Librarian, Library Attendant |
| Laboratories | Lab Staff | Lab Assistant, Lab Attendant, Computer Lab Technician |
| Sports & Co-curricular | Sports Staff | Sports Coordinator, Sports Coach, Yoga Instructor, Self Defence Instructor |

| Human Resources | HR Staff | HR Manager, HR Executive, Payroll Executive |

Notes:
- PRT, TGT and PGT are the standard Indian teaching grades: Primary Teacher, Trained Graduate Teacher (about classes 6–10) and Post Graduate Teacher (senior secondary).
- Government schools (KVS / NVS) use these office post names: Junior / Senior Secretariat Assistant (formerly LDC / UDC), Stenographer, Lab Attendant, Multi-Tasking Staff and Sub Staff.
- Private, CBSE and boarding schools add Admission Counsellor, Activity Teachers, Special Educator, Hostel Warden / Matron, Nurse, IT Manager and Estate Manager.
