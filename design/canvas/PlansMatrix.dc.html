Pricing & packaging

Four plans, one codebase — switched by configuration

Each plan is a saved set of feature switches and limits. Anything one tier up can be sold as an add-on, so a 200-student school can still buy Payroll without moving plan.

Suggested price model

Base: price per enrolled student per year, with a yearly minimum per plan.
Add-ons: flat yearly price per school (for example Payroll, Online payments, Power BI).
Usage: SMS / WhatsApp over the monthly credit billed per message.
Upgrade trigger: enrolment above the plan's cap prompts the next plan; nothing is switched off mid-year.
